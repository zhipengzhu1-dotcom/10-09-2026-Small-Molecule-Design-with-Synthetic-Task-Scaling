# 09 — What replaces the IPO loss and the async setup?

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 01
Map: [Synthetic Task Scaling at <8B](../map.md)

## Question

The paper's optimiser does not exist in the tooling available here. What is the faithful
substitute, and does the substitution actually matter?

The paper uses **prime-rl's IPO loss** (`ipo_loss_fn`) — DPPO-Binary-TV plus the Kimi K2.5
regularisation term, with token-level log importance ratios clipped to ±10 and a trust-region
mask. TRL and Unsloth expose **`grpo` / `dr_grpo` / `dapo` / `bnpo`**, not IPO.

The key observation to test: **IPO exists to cope with trainer–inference mismatch**, which is a
consequence of *asynchronous* training — the paper's inference workers run ahead of the trainer,
so later tokens in a rollout may be generated by a newer policy than earlier ones, and they
allow up to 2 steps of staleness. On a single GPU with colocated generation and training, that
mismatch largely disappears, and with it IPO's reason to exist.

Resolve:

1. **Loss.** Is `dr_grpo` the right substitute? The paper's advantage estimator is explicitly
   **Dr. GRPO-style — mean-centred within group, no std normalisation** (Eq 6), which matches
   `dr_grpo` directly. Confirm what Unsloth's `loss_type` options actually compute (its default
   is `dapo`, which is *not* what the paper does) and pick deliberately rather than by default.
2. **Sync vs async.** Decide to train synchronously. If so, document that the staleness
   parameter (`max_off_policy_steps = 2`) and the importance-ratio clipping become no-ops, and
   that this is a *simplification* rather than an infidelity. If any asynchrony remains — e.g.
   because vLLM generation and the trainer overlap — say what mismatch survives.
3. **The KL coefficient.** The paper sets β = 1e-3, but in IPO that β multiplies a
   trainer–inference disagreement penalty, **not** a KL-to-reference term. Under `dr_grpo`, β is
   an ordinary KL penalty against the reference policy. These are different quantities wearing
   the same letter — decide the value on its own merits (β = 0.0 is common for GRPO and saves a
   reference-model forward pass and its VRAM) and record that the paper's 1e-3 does not
   transfer.
4. **The remaining Table A2 values** — AdamW lr 1e-6, betas (0.9, 0.999), wd 0.01, grad clip
   0.1, constant LR with no warmup or decay, temperature 1.0. Decide which carry over unchanged
   and which must move. In particular, lr 1e-6 was chosen for full fine-tuning of a 35B MoE; if
   this reproduction uses LoRA, the appropriate learning rate is typically one to two orders of
   magnitude higher, and keeping 1e-6 would mean training that silently does nothing.
5. **LoRA vs full fine-tune** — the paper does not state which it used. Resolve it here using
   ticket 01's measured VRAM, and if LoRA, decide rank and target modules. (This also clears the
   corresponding fog item on the map.)
